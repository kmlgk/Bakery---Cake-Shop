Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, customizing, and picking up your cake.

Ordering

Standard cakes need 5 days notice. Wedding and multi-tier custom cakes should be booked 4–6 weeks ahead, especially during peak season (May–October).

We occasionally accommodate 48-hour rush orders for simpler designs, subject to a rush fee. Call the studio directly rather than using the online form for these.

Submit our custom cake enquiry form with your occasion, servings, and design details. We reply with a fixed quote within 24 hours.

Customization

Delivery & Pickup

We deliver within a 15-mile radius of Riverdale for a flat fee. Longer distances may be arranged for wedding cakes — ask when you submit your enquiry.

Contact us at least 48 hours before your original date and we'll do our best to accommodate the change, subject to availability.

Payment

A 30% non-refundable deposit confirms your order and locks in your pickup date. The remaining balance is due at pickup or delivery.

We accept all major credit cards, Apple Pay, and bank transfer. Cash is welcome for in-store pickup orders.
