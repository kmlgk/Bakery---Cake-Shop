How it started

A Kitchen Table, a Wedding Cake, and a Waitlist

In 2011, Elena Moreau baked her sister's wedding cake on a borrowed kitchen table. Guests asked for her number before the first slice was cut — and Bloom & Batter was born three weeks later out of a rented storefront on Magnolia Avenue.

Today our team of six decorators still hand-sketches every custom order, still bakes in small batches every morning, and still believes the best cakes come from listening closely to the story behind each celebration.
